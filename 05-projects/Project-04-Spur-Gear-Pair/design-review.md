# Design Review — Project 04

## Project

Spur Gear Pair — Version 1

## Review Status

Completed

---

## Overall Assessment

The project successfully demonstrates the basic CAD construction of a two-gear system and introduces fundamental mechanical transmission concepts.

The use of two different tooth counts creates a clear theoretical gear ratio and makes the project more engineering-focused than a simple standalone CAD object.

---

## Strengths

### 1. Parametric Design

The Tinkercad Useful Gear generator was used to create the gears, allowing parameters such as tooth count, pitch, bore diameter, and height to be controlled.

### 2. Consistent Components

Both gears use:

- Pitch: 2
- Height: 5 mm
- Bore: 6 mm
- Slop: 0.01

This creates consistency between the two components.

### 3. Different Tooth Counts

The 20T and 40T gears create a simple 2:1 theoretical gear ratio.

### 4. Measurement

The Ruler was used in midpoint mode to measure the center-to-center position of the gears.

Measured value:

**38 mm**

### 5. Engineering Documentation

The project records the design parameters, calculations, CAD process, limitations, and possible future improvements.

---

## Areas for Improvement

### Mechanical Validation

The gears have not been physically printed or tested.

A future version should test:

- Actual tooth engagement
- Shaft fit
- Rotation
- Friction
- Clearance
- Load

### Motion Demonstration

Tinkercad 3D Design was used for CAD modeling rather than mechanical motion simulation.

A future project could demonstrate the gears using a physical mechanism or appropriate simulation software.

### Manufacturing Considerations

Future versions should consider:

- 3D-printing tolerances
- Material selection
- Shaft diameter
- Gear thickness
- Tooth strength
- Clearance

---

## Engineering Evaluation

The project demonstrates progression from basic CAD modeling toward mechanical engineering design.

It combines:

**CAD + Measurement + Geometry + Mechanical Concepts + Mathematics**

This represents an important step toward more advanced mechanical engineering projects.
