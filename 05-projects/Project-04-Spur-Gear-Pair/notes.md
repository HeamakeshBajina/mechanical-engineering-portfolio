# Project 04 — Engineering Notes

## Date

September 2026

## Project

Spur Gear Pair — Version 1

---

## Goal

Create a pair of spur gears in Tinkercad and learn the basic mechanical concepts behind gear systems.

---

## Gear A

- Teeth: 20
- Pitch: 2
- Height: 5 mm
- Bore: 6 mm
- Slop: 0.01

Role:

Driver gear

---

## Gear B

- Teeth: 40
- Pitch: 2
- Height: 5 mm
- Bore: 6 mm
- Slop: 0.01

Role:

Driven gear

---

## Positioning

The gears were placed on the same horizontal centerline.

The Tinkercad Ruler was used in midpoint mode to measure the distance between their centers.

Measured center-to-center distance:

**38 mm**

The gears were positioned so that their teeth visually meet.

---

## Gear Ratio Calculation

Gear ratio:

40 / 20 = 2

Therefore:

**Gear ratio = 2:1**

If the 20-tooth gear is the driver:

- Driver speed = 1×
- Driven speed = 0.5×

The theoretical torque relationship is approximately the inverse of the speed relationship, assuming an ideal system with no losses.

## Key Concepts Learned

### Bore

The central hole through which a shaft can pass.

### Pitch

A parameter used by the Tinkercad gear generator to define aspects of the gear tooth geometry and spacing.

### Slop

A small clearance parameter used by the gear generator.

### Tooth Count

The number of teeth on a gear. It determines the rotational relationship between meshing gears.

## Reflection

This project introduced mechanical power transmission through a simple gear pair.

The most important concept learned was that gear ratios can be determined from tooth counts rather than simply comparing the physical sizes of the gears.

The project also demonstrated the importance of measuring CAD geometry instead of relying entirely on visual positioning.

---

## Limitations

The gear pair was not physically printed or tested.

The gears were positioned visually to demonstrate meshing, but actual mechanical performance was not measured.
