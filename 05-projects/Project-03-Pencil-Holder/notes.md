# Project 03 Notes – Engineering Pencil Holder

## Date

August 2026

---

## Project Goal

Create a simple functional pencil holder while practicing accurate dimensions, hollow geometry, wall thickness, alignment, and basic engineering design.

---

## Starting Point

This project was created after completing the four introductory CAD lessons:

1. Interface and Navigation
2. Basic Shapes and Object Manipulation
3. Alignment, Grouping and Holes
4. Precision Engineering

---

## Design Specifications

- Outer diameter: 70 mm
- Inner diameter: 64 mm
- Overall height: 100 mm
- Wall thickness: 3 mm
- Base thickness: 5 mm

---

## Calculation

Wall thickness:

```text
(70 − 64) / 2 = 3 mm
```

Base thickness:

```text
100 − 95 = 5 mm
```

The inner hole was therefore modeled with a height of 95 mm.

---

## CAD Tools Practiced

- Cylinder
- Hole
- Align
- Group

---

## What I Learned

I learned how a simple solid cylinder can be converted into a functional hollow component by using subtractive geometry.

I also learned that wall thickness must be deliberately designed rather than created randomly.

The Align tool was important for ensuring that the internal cavity remained concentric with the outer body.

---

## Engineering Thinking

The main engineering decision in this project was balancing material usage with structural requirements.

Instead of creating a completely solid cylinder, material was removed from the center while maintaining a 3 mm wall and 5 mm base.

This creates a functional container while reducing unnecessary material.

---

## Challenges

The main challenges were:

- Setting the correct internal diameter.
- Maintaining consistent wall thickness.
- Creating the correct base thickness.
- Centering the internal cavity.

---

## How I Solved Them

I used exact dimensions with the Ruler tool and used the Align tool to center the internal geometry.

I also checked the difference between the outer and inner diameters to verify the wall thickness.

---

## Reflection

This project was my first exercise involving a deliberately designed wall thickness and internal cavity.

It helped me understand that CAD modeling is not only about making an object look correct. Dimensions and geometry must be selected for a reason.

---

## Future Improvements

For a future version, I could investigate:

- Reducing material usage further.
- Adding structural ribs.
- Adding compartments.
- Improving the edge geometry.
- Designing the holder for a specific manufacturing process.
