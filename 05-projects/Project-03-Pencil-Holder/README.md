# Project 03 – Engineering Pencil Holder

## Objective

Design and model a functional pencil holder using Autodesk Tinkercad while applying fundamental CAD and engineering design principles learned in previous lessons.

The project focuses on creating a hollow cylindrical structure with controlled wall thickness, accurate dimensions, and a stable base.

---

## Project Overview

The pencil holder was designed as a simple cylindrical container capable of holding pencils and other writing instruments.

The design uses two concentric cylinders:

- An outer solid cylinder forming the main body.
- An inner hole removing material and creating the internal cavity.

The inner cylinder was intentionally shorter than the outer cylinder to leave material at the bottom of the holder.

---

## Design Specifications

| Feature | Dimension |
|---|---:|
| Outer Diameter | 70 mm |
| Inner Diameter | 64 mm |
| Overall Height | 100 mm |
| Wall Thickness | 3 mm |
| Base Thickness | 5 mm |

### Wall Thickness Calculation

The wall thickness is calculated from the difference between the outer and inner diameters:

```text
Wall thickness = (Outer diameter − Inner diameter) / 2

Wall thickness = (70 − 64) / 2

Wall thickness = 3 mm
```

## Engineering Concepts Applied

### Dimensional Accuracy

Exact dimensions were used instead of positioning the geometry by eye.

### Wall Thickness

A 3 mm wall was created by using a 64 mm internal diameter inside a 70 mm external diameter.

### Internal Cavity

A shorter cylindrical hole was used to remove internal material while leaving a solid base.

### Symmetry

The internal cavity was aligned concentrically with the outer cylinder.

### Material Usage

The hollow design reduces unnecessary material compared with a completely solid cylinder.

### Design for Manufacturing

The geometry was designed as a simple cylindrical component suitable for basic additive manufacturing.

---

## Design Process

1. Created an outer cylinder with a diameter of 70 mm and height of 100 mm.
2. Created a second cylinder with a diameter of 64 mm.
3. Converted the inner cylinder into a Hole.
4. Set the inner hole height to 95 mm to leave a 5 mm base.
5. Aligned the two cylinders concentrically.
6. Grouped the solid and hole to create the hollow holder.
7. Checked the final dimensions using the Ruler tool.
8. Inspected the model from multiple viewpoints.

---

## Design Decisions

The 70 mm outer diameter provides a reasonably stable cylindrical footprint.

The 64 mm internal diameter creates a 3 mm wall thickness, providing a balance between material usage and structural rigidity for a basic prototype.

The 5 mm base provides a solid bottom surface while maintaining the internal cavity.

---

## Challenges

- Creating a correctly sized internal cavity.
- Maintaining a consistent wall thickness.
- Ensuring that the inner cylinder was centered correctly.
- Leaving sufficient material at the bottom of the holder.

---

## How I Solved Them

- Used the Ruler tool to verify dimensions.
- Used the Align tool to center the inner cylinder.
- Used a shorter hole cylinder to create the required base thickness.
- Used Group to combine the solid and subtractive geometry.
