# Project 02 Notes – Engineering Phone Stand

## Date

5 August 2026

---

## Time Spent

Approximately 10 minutes

---

## Project Goal

Create a functional phone stand capable of securely supporting a smartphone while applying the CAD tools learned during the foundational lessons.

---

## What I Learned

- Structural support improves stability.
- Support angle affects usability.
- Accurate alignment creates cleaner designs.
- Simple designs are often stronger and easier to manufacture.
- CAD precision improves the overall quality of the final model.

---

## Challenges Faced

- Determining the correct support angle.
- Balancing stability with material usage.
- Positioning all components accurately.

---

## How I Solved Them

- Used the Align tool.
- Used the Ruler tool.
- Tested different support positions before choosing the final design.

---

## Future Improvements

- Add rounded edges.
- Include a charging cable slot.
- Reduce unnecessary material.
- Increase the base length.

---

## Reflection

This project helped me move from practicing CAD tools to designing a functional engineering product. It demonstrated how stability, dimensions, and support geometry affect the usability of a real object.
